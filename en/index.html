About Us

An Angolan Company
with Industrial Vision

Ango Terras LP – Comércio Industrial (SU), Lda is a company incorporated under Angolan law, focused on the industrial sector, duly established in Luanda and registered at the Commercial Registry Office, represented by its CEO, Leonel Manuel Pinto.

Specialised in the development, planning and implementation of large-scale industrial projects, we work to drive the industrialisation of the agricultural sector, strengthen the value chain and promote the sustainable development of Angola.

Strategic Partnership

Joint Venture with
Midwest Capital Brasil

The project results from a strategic partnership with Midwest Capital Investimentos e Participações Ltda (Midwest Brasil), formalised through a joint venture agreement specifically directed at the industrial development of Angola.

This cooperation combines technical know-how, operational capacity and international experience, creating a solid foundation for the implementation of modern industrial infrastructure, technology transfer and training of local workforce.
